About Me

I've always been drawn to building things - whether it's a website, an app, or a system that actually solves a problem. That instinct turned into a career: web development, SEO, digital marketing, and founding my own ventures, across nonprofits, startups, and client projects. I move fast, I figure things out, and I don't wait to be told what to do.

What I do is pretty straightforward: understand what a business needs online, build it clean, and make sure it gets found. WordPress, Laravel, SEO, ad campaigns - whatever it takes to get results. Websites ranked on Google within 24 hours of launch. Apps built and published.

When I'm not coding, I'm editing videos, planning the next trip, or growing something in the garden.
